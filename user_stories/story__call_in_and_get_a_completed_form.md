# Call in and get a completed form

## Story

As an applicant who does not read English well, I can call Zuzu, say what I need
in my own words, and end the call with a completed USCIS form to review and sign.

## Why this one is first

Everything else is in service of this. If the call works and the form comes out
right, the architecture behind it is a detail; if it does not, nothing else
counts.

## Acceptance

- The agent asks what I need and confirms the form by its plain-English title
  before starting -- "Application for Employment Authorization, Form I-765" --
  never a bare form number, which means nothing to the person filing it.
- Every question is asked one at a time, in the language I am speaking.
- Names, dates and numbers are read back to me before they are stored.
- I can say I do not know an answer, and the call continues rather than stalling.
- When the last question is answered the completed PDF is produced, with the
  signature fields left empty.

## Not in scope

Submitting the form, paying the fee, or signing for the applicant. Zuzu fills to
the review step and stops there.
