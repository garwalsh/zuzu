# Call in and get a completed form

As an applicant who does not read English well, I can call Zuzu, say what I need in my own words, and end the call with a completed USCIS form to review and sign.

Acceptance:
- The agent asks what I need and confirms the form by its plain-English title before starting, never by form number alone.
- Every question is asked one at a time, in the language I am speaking.
- Names, dates and numbers are read back to me before they are stored.
- I can say I do not know an answer and the call continues.
- When the last question is answered the completed PDF is produced, unfilled signature fields intact.
